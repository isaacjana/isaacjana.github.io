Keep little hands inside the app

  • Install it: use your browser's “Add to Home Screen” / “Install app”. It then opens full screen and works offline.
  • iPhone / iPad: Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access → turn on. Open the app and triple-click the side (or Home) button to lock the screen to this app.
  • Android: Settings → Security (or Security & privacy) → More settings → App pinning → turn on. Open Recent apps, tap the app icon, then Pin.
  • Turn the device sideways (landscape) for the best fit.
