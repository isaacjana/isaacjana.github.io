Tips

  • Install it: use your browser's “Add to Home Screen” / “Install app”. It then opens full screen and works offline.
  • Turn pages: swipe, or tap the big arrow buttons.
  • Pictures are toys too: tap the animals, moon, balloons and bubbles.
  • Keep little hands inside the app: iPhone / iPad: Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access. Android: Settings → Security → App pinning.
