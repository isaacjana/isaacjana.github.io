# Ocean Web App - User Manual

Welcome to the Ocean Web App, a comprehensive solution for managing live seafood stocks, orders, and deliveries.

## 1. Getting Started

### Accessing the App
Navigate to the web application URL. You will be greeted by the landing page.

### Signing In
- Click the **"Sign in with Google"** button.
- Choose your Google account to log in.
- **First Time Login**: You will be automatically registered as a **Client**. If you require Admin or Driver access, please contact the business owner to update your role.

---

## 2. For Clients (Restaurants & Individuals)

### Viewing Live Stock
- Upon logging in, you will see the **Live Seafood Catalog**.
- Browse available products, check live prices, and see real-time quantity availability.

### Placing an Order
- Click **"Add to Order"** on any item.
- Go to your cart (Floating icon) to review and submit your order.
- You can track your order status in the **"My Orders"** section.

---

## 3. For Business Admins

### Dashboard Analytics
- View monthly revenue, active orders count, and stock summary at a glance.

### Managing Stock
- Navigate to **"Live Stock"**.
- **Add Stock**: Click "+ Add Stock", enter details (Name, Supplier, Price, Unit, Qty).
- **Update**: Edit existing stock levels or prices.
- **Delete**: Remove items no longer sold.

### Managing Orders
- View incoming orders in the **"Orders"** tab.
- Assign orders to drivers (if logical assignment is implemented) or simply update status to "Ready for Delivery".
- Generate LHDN-compliant invoices for completed orders.

---

## 4. For Drivers

### Picking Up Jobs
- Go to the **"Available Jobs"** tab.
- View list of orders ready for delivery.
- Click **"Pick Job"** to assign it to yourself.

### Delivery
- Navigate to **"My Deliveries"**.
- View delivery address and details.
- Mark order as **"Completed"** once delivered.

---

## 5. Frequency & Maintenance
- **Stocks**: Update stock counts daily or as new suppliers arrive.
- **Invoices**: Generate invoices immediately upon order completion.
- **Data**: All data is securely stored in the cloud.

For technical support, please contact the developer.
