Signs of Labor (Tanda Bersalin)

Turun Tanda (Bloody Show)

Mucus discharge mixed with a little blood. This means your cervix is starting to open. Start timing contractions!

Pecah Ketuban (Water Breaking)

Gush or leak of clear fluid. Go to the hospital immediately even if there is no pain yet.
